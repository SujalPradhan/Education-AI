## Flags

1. **C5b, needs action:** the state toggles show 6 states with AI+CS PD funding (AR, IN, LA, NJ, OH, VA), but the report's headline says 5. Cite either "5 per the headline" or "6 per the state pages", and do not mix them.
2. **C10c, needs action:** the student activation rate printed as 62.4% computes to 63.1% from the printed counts. The printed 45.9% "MAU" uses activated accounts as its denominator. Against invited students, MAU is 29.0%.
3. **C4:** the Western+Southern vs other comparison is not significant (7/21 vs 5/17, p = 1.0). Do not present "predominantly western and southern Europe" as a statistical finding.
4. **C7:** the student sample sizes and the wave behind N = 1,332 are not internally consistent. The 35% district-leader figure comes from a fall-2024 survey, not spring 2025.
5. **C3:** inside the Eurydice report, the CPD count for safe/ethical AI is 16 in the executive summary and 15 in chapter 5.
6. **C12:** the 2018–19 national access baseline (45%) covers only 39 states.
