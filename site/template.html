Computer science and AI in schools: US vs Europe

How often does the CS curriculum change?

US

3–5× more often than other subjects

National school standards, rewrites per decade [1]–[8]

EUROPE

7 in 10 systems were rewriting it at once

39 education systems, 2020/21 [13]

Is CS part of what schools must teach?

US

6 → 44 states set CS standards in 8 years

States with K-12 CS standards [9]

EUROPE

Taught in 9 in 10 systems by age 11–14

Informatics as its own discipline, 39 systems, 2020/21 [13]

Is AI being taught in schools?

US

To some students. Rarely to all.

US high schools, 2024–25 [10] [11]

EUROPE

In 1 in 3 national curricula

AI in the lower-secondary curriculum, 38 systems, 2024/25 [14]

Does teaching AI work?

Unknown. Not one rigorous study, anywhere.

Stanford review of AI in K-12 education, 2026 [12]. Europe's largest test (Estonia, 9,000+ students) has not reported yet [15]
